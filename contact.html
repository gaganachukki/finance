Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

You can create an account by clicking the "Login" button and selecting "Create Account". The process is entirely digital and takes less than 5 minutes.

How can I track my investments?

Once logged in, navigate to the Investments tab on your dashboard. You'll see real-time performance metrics and detailed reports.

How does the EMI calculator work?

The EMI calculator uses standard financial formulas to compute your monthly payments based on loan amount, interest rate, and tenure.

How can I secure my account?

We strongly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) in your account settings under the Security tab.

How can I contact customer support?

You can reach us via the contact form on this page, or email us directly at support@finora.com.
